Privacy on the modern internet is a fucking second job. You install an adblocker, you flip a few switches, you try to keep third-party junk out of the browser, and many pages just spins forever because some tracking script didn't get its little dopamine hit.

<!--more-->

Most of the internet now seems to be built on surveillance and most content is bait. The real product is our habits, our interests, our location, our devices, or our "likely to buy" score. If they can't stick a third-party cookie on you, they'll certainly stitch you together with fingerprinting.

Fingerprinting is the part that makes me want to throw my laptop out the window (I'm kidding...). It's a pile of them like canvas and WebGL quirks, installed fonts, screen size, time zone, audio stack oddities, browser APIs, and the fact that your IP address is basically a name tag unless you're constantly hopping networks. Most apps in our phones serve as fingerprinting tools.

I'm burning out. The more I care, the more I get frustrated.

You harden your browser and your bank treats you like a criminal or half of the web stops working as it should.

And the privacy crowd sometimes makes it worse. I'm mean, just… unrealistic. Not everyone have the knowledge or patience to use separate browsers, separate profiles, separate email aliases, separate numbers, separate everything. 

The privacy stuff is a small act of refusal in a system designed to wear you down. That's why fighting back sometimes is harder than giving up, though.
