Samsung makes great hardware and then ruins the experience with software.

I’m not saying this as some Apple fanboy. I left iPhone for Samsung about a year ago because I wanted more control and more flexibility. Android is supposed to be the “open” option.
<!--more-->

Reality has been a lot less sexy with slow updates and a pile of pre-installed junk I never asked for.

Waiting for Android updates as a Samsung user is like watching a bus schedule that’s written in invisible ink. Google announces a new version, everyone talks about security improvements, and then you sit there for weeks or months like a sucker.

Meanwhile iPhone users get updates basically immediately. That’s the part that’s embarrassing. Samsung has the money, the talent, and the scale. And still the update cadence feels like an afterthought.

Yes, I get why. One UI sits on top of Android, so every update has to be adapted, tested, and rolled out. Fine.

This isn’t just me being impatient. Security patches matter. Personal data is under constant attack, and I shouldn’t have to wonder whether my phone is months behind on fixes.

Then there’s the bloatware. Two browsers. Two app stores. A pile of Samsung apps I didn’t request. And the best part is that a bunch of it can’t be properly removed. It eats storage. It runs in the background. It drains battery. Samsung thinks it owns your phone more than you do. If people want the Samsung suite, great. Make it opt-in. Let me uninstall it. Let me actually disable it. Give me choice.

What I want is the option for clean Android, a "pure Android" experience. Stock Android is simpler, faster, and gets updates quicker because there’s less vendor nonsense in the way. Even from a privacy standpoint, fewer layers usually means fewer places for tracking to hide.

It shouldn’t be too much to ask for a company this big to do better. At minimum, be transparent. Tell people when updates are coming. Don’t make customers guess.

Samsung’s hardware is undeniably great. Hardware alone isn’t enough.

If the updates stay slow and the bloatware stays glued to the device, the message sent is that our time doesn’t matter. And I’m done paying premium money for that.
